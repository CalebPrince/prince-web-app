## Video direction

- Palette: near-black `cream` canvas (#020403), electric-green `fire-orange` accent (#62FF98), muted green ink (#7BBF91), and cyan-muted support (#5BE3FF), exactly as remixed in frame.md.
- Type: Inter display at 850–900 with tight tracking; Cascadia Code/mono-style uppercase labels. One dominant display moment per frame.
- Motion grammar: VO-paced reveals on long-tail power3-style settles; hard kinetic swaps, controlled push-throughs, and status-lock pulses. Reveal each object only when its phrase arrives. Holds allow only subtle deterministic signal jitter.
- Rhythm: Frames 1–3 accelerate; Frame 4 briefly holds the proof surfaces for inspection; Frame 5 resolves into the longest static URL read.
- Safe area: all essential copy and UI remain in the top 83% and centered mobile-safe column.
- Never: purple-blue AI gradients, rounded glassmorphism, floating bokeh, browser chrome, front-loaded slideshow motion, or independent screensaver drift.
format: 1080x1920
duration: 30s
message: "Prince Caleb builds real AI systems that are already running, not vague concepts"
arc: "Demo Loop — claim → proof → workflow → operating system → CTA"
audience: businesses with repetitive customer and operations workflows
mode: autonomous
music: fast electronic tech pulse, confident and percussive
