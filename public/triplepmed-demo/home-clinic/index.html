How It Works

Care at home, in four steps

From your first call to follow-up, everything is coordinated by the same Triple P team that runs our Accra clinic.

1

Tell us what you need

Book online or message us on WhatsApp. We'll ask a few questions to understand the level of care required.

2

We schedule your visit

You pick the window that suits you. We confirm the clinician assigned and what to expect.

3

Your clinician arrives

A licensed nurse or physician arrives with the equipment needed and carries out the visit in your home.

4

Follow-up and records

Findings go into your Triple P record, so any follow-up, lab work, or telehealth review picks up where the visit left off.

What our caregivers do

Practical, dignified support delivered in your own home — around the clock where it's needed.

Companionship

Presence, conversation, and company through the day so no one is left on their own.

Standby assistance

A steady hand nearby for moving, transferring, and getting around safely.

Personal care

Supervision and assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting.

Medication reminders

Medication, treatment, and exercise reminders kept on schedule, every day.

Meal preparation

Light housekeeping & laundry

Running errands
