How It Works

Three steps to see a doctor from home

1. Book online

Choose "Virtual / Telehealth" on our booking form and tell us what you'd like to discuss.

2. Get confirmed

Our team confirms your appointment time and sends a secure video link by email or WhatsApp.

3. Join the call

Click the link at your appointment time to join from your phone, tablet, or computer. No app required.

Why Go Virtual

Real care, without the commute

  • Skip travel and waiting-room time entirely
  • Ideal for follow-ups, prescription renewals, and minor concerns
  • Private, secure video connection, not a public app
  • Same clinical team as our in-person clinic
