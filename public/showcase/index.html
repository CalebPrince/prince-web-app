03 — What I can do for your business

Work that runs itself.

Most of what a small team does all day is the same job over and over: answering the same questions, copying the same details between apps, chasing the same follow-ups. That work does not need a person — it needs a system. I build the system, and your team gets back to the work that actually needs them.

01

AI voice & chat agents

A trained agent that picks up the phone and answers WhatsApp around the clock, in your business’s own words — so nothing goes unanswered at 11pm, on a Sunday, or while your line is busy.

  • Answers calls and messages 24/7, never engaged, never on hold
  • Qualifies enquiries and captures every detail you need
  • Books straight into your calendar and confirms with the customer
  • Passes the conversation to a human the moment it needs one
02

Business process automation

The tools you already pay for, wired together so the work moves between them on its own. No one re-typing an order into a spreadsheet, no one remembering to send the reminder.

  • Enquiries land in your CRM already sorted and assigned
  • Bookings turn themselves into invoices, receipts and reminders
  • Missed leads get followed up automatically, within minutes
  • Daily and weekly reports arrive without anyone building them
03

Websites, web platforms & mobile apps

Fast, properly built sites and applications shaped around how your customers and your staff actually work — not a template you have to work around.

  • Business, e-commerce and booking websites
  • Internal platforms: orders, records, stock, staff, dashboards
  • iOS and Android apps for customers or field teams
  • Payments, and integration with the systems you already run
04

Social media set-up & management

The same service behind the pages above: I build the page, establish the look and the posting rhythm, run it until it has momentum, then hand it to your team trained and documented.

  • Facebook and Instagram pages set up and branded properly
  • Content calendar, posts, reels and captions
  • Replies and community handling, with the routine parts automated
  • A clean handover so your team can carry it on

Hands off by default. Human only when it truly matters.

Automation should not mean your customers get stuck talking to a machine. Every system I build runs on its own until it reaches something that genuinely needs a person — a complaint, a price that needs approving, a decision only you should make — and then it hands over cleanly, with the full history attached.
